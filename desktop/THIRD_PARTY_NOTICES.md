# Material Icon Theme

Conflux bundles SVG icons and generated filename/folder associations from
[Material Icon Theme](https://github.com/material-extensions/vscode-material-icon-theme),
version 5.40.0, under the MIT license.

The complete upstream license is copied without modification to
`public/material-icons/LICENSE.txt` during asset preparation and included at
`material-icons/LICENSE.txt` in the built application. The dependency is pinned
in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`.
